Overview

A home built on open sides and settled ground

SV Anasuya Apartments brings 55 premium 2 & 3 BHK residences to Balaji Nagar, behind Lecturers Colony in Hayathnagar. Every block is planned 100% Vaastu compliant, with no common walls between units — three sides open to light, air and view, rather than the single shared-wall layouts common elsewhere.

The project is currently ongoing, with handover targeted for December 2027.
